## Master Multitasking: Manage Up To 8 MIDI/DJ Controllers Simultaneously Using the DEX 3 & PCDJ Software

[![Use Multiple DJ Controllers With DEX 3](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/multiplecontrollers-coverimage.jpg?resize=706%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/multiplecontrollers-coverimage.jpg?fit=706%2C360&ssl=1 "Using Multiple DJ Controllers")

DEX 3 **DJ and video mixing software** has plug-and-play [support for over 85 DJ controllers](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) right now with additional controller support added regularly — _but did you know you could use up to 8 Midi/DJ controllers simultaneously?_

[![Pioneer D](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/DDJ-SB2.png?fit=300%2C215&ssl=1 "Pioneer DDJ-SB2 Top Angled")](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/DDJ-SB2.png?fit=1030%2C737&ssl=1)

The ability to **use multiple DJ controllers** at once is one lesser known but truly awesome feature of DEX 3.

Controllers allow for tactile control over DEX 3\. They are essentially an extension of the DJ software’s feature-set. **For a hand-on mixing DJ there’s no debate; you will need to use a DJ controller** during live performance.

Support for **multiple DJ/Midi controllers** at once can provide you with a whole new level of control. For example, use your plug-and-play [Pioneer DDJ-SB2](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) to control all the basic mixing features of DEX 3, but map (using our easy-to-use [MIDI LEARN](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) feature) a basic pad-based midi controller such as the [Samson Graphite MD13](http://www.samsontech.com/samson/products/usb-midi/controllers/graphite-md13/) to DEX 3’s 8 sample decks to trigger samples on the fly for a little finger drumming action.

[![Gemini G2V DJ controller angled](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/G2V-Angled-1-893x490.jpg?fit=300%2C165&ssl=1 "Gemini G2V DJ controller")](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/G2V-Angled-1-893x490.jpg?fit=893%2C490&ssl=1)

DEX 3 allows you to [connect and disconnect DJ controllers on the fly](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), even while mixing. So you can **connect/disconnect up to 8 DJ controllers in real-time** without missing a beat.

Another reason you may want to have **multiple DJ controllers** connected is for redundancy; If you have a secondary DJ controller plugged in you also have an **instant backup** to switch over to in the event a controller dies on your mid set.

You can check out our list of [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) supported DJ controllers [HERE](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/). Check back often as additional DJ controllers are added all the time.
