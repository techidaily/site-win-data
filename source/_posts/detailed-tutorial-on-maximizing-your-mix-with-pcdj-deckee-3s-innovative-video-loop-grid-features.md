## Detailed Tutorial on Maximizing Your Mix with PCDJ Deckee 3'S Innovative Video Loop Grid Features

[![](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/dex3gridcoverimage.jpg?resize=530%2C270&ssl=1)](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/dex3gridcoverimage.jpg?fit=530%2C298&ssl=1 "dex3gridcoverimage")

**Our plan is to launch a release candidate public pre-release of our much anticipate PCDJ DEX 3 [DJ mixing software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) in the next few days. In the meantime, we’re keeping the teaser videos coming with this demonstration of the new Beat and Loop grid included in the new default 4-deck skin.**

If you haven’t taken note, DEX 3 is an all-new platform upgrade for our flagship DJ mixing software DEX 2\. It’s been in the works for the last year, and boasts a new audio engine, effects engine, graphics engine and video mixing engine. The only core component carrying over from DEX 2 is our custom (and fantastic sounding) audio engine. This upgrade (FREE for current DEX 2 DJ software owners) is the foundation for our next 5+ years of development – so after DEX 3 launches our users can expect many more updates throughout the coarse of this year and beyond.

In this short video we showcase the new effects grid that is a focal point of the new audio tab. The “virtual LED’s” provide you with a visual reference point to quickly ascertain what effects are applied per deck, and if that deck is in a loop or not. In the video we show you how this works with a mouse, but the grid works equally well if you’re using any of the [supported DJ controllers](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/).
