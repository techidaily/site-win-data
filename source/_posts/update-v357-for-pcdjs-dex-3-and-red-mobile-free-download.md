**Download update 3.5.7 for DEX 3 and RED Mobile 3 DJ mixing software applications.** 

_Fresh out of the bat-cave:_ version 3.5.7 has just been made available for both [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) and [RED Mobile 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/). Included in 3.5.7 is an updated Pulselocker SDK, providing users an even better experience when using the music subscription in-app. You’ll also notice a broader music selection as the Pulselocker team has been busy ingesting loads of music so rapidly that their servers music be suffering a serious bellyache. Now is a great time test drive [Pulselocker](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) integration for 14 days, free of charge.

[![](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/rm3-newshot.jpg?fit=300%2C169&ssl=1 "RED Mobile 3 screen shot new")](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/rm3-newshot.jpg?fit=1030%2C579&ssl=1)

Two new [DJ controller](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) maps are also included with the 3.5.7 installer; the [Pioneer DDJ-SB2](http://www.pioneerdj.com/en-us/product/controller/ddj-sb2/black/overview/) and the [Reloop RMP-4](http://www.reloop.com/reloop-rmp-4). Both are now “_zero-configure_” with our DJ software offerings. More choices for tactile, hands-on control over your PCDJ performance.

Take one of the new 3.5.7 updates for a test spin [HERE](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)

#### DJ Software Version 3.5.7 Changes And Improvements

* New Pulselocker SDK Implemented
* Pioneer DDJ-SB2 Map Included
* Reloop RMP-4 Map Included
* – New DJ Controller scripting actions: “deckLoopMode”, “reloadMidi” (similar to “reloadSkin” but for controllers development)  
– New DJ Controller scripting notification: global: “loopMode” (0 vinyl mode, 1 slip mode)
* Other small optimizations
