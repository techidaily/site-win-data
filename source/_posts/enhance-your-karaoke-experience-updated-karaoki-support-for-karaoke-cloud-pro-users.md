Please download and install from the button below. This free Karaoki update will resolve any issues you may have trying to log into the Karaoke Cloud Pro subscription.

Also, if you missed last weeks notice, the Karaoke Cloud Pro subscription price-per-month has been reduced to only $19.99, making it the most affordable commercially legal karaoke subscription available. [Read the announcement](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) from Digitrax CEO Joe Vangieri.

NOTE: When updating your important data, such as karaoke cases, singers history, etc., will all remain intact. Simply download and install with your existing version of Karaoki closed to update automatically.
