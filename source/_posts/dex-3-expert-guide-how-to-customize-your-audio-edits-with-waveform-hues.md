## DEX 3 Expert Guide: How To Customize Your Audio Edits With Waveform Hues

[![Editing Waveform colors in DJ software](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/editingwaveformcolors-dex3coverimage.jpg?resize=530%2C298&ssl=1)](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/editingwaveformcolors-dex3coverimage.jpg?fit=530%2C298&ssl=1 "editing waveform colors DEX 3")

So you’re not a fan of the default [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) waveform default colors? No worries, you can pop the hood yourself and **customize each independent deck waveform color**.

[LEARN MORE ABOUT DEX 3 ![PCDJ DEX 3 Logo and Icon](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/DEXlogo.png?fit=300%2C300&ssl=1 "PCDJ DEX 3 Logo")](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)

**DEX 3.5’s skin is remarkably versatile**, with various modes including 2-decks, 4-decks, video mixing panels, and sample decks – so it’s already easy to make DEX 3 your own by selecting the mode that works best for your DJ needs. You can also [download DEX 3 Skins](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) to personalize the look even more so. For advanced designers, DEX 3 skins can be created from a personal design using the [PCDJ Skin Designer](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/); the only true limitation is the creators imagination.

The default DEX 3 skins showcase our default waveform colors of green and orange – with the 4-deck version equipped with blue and red for decks 3 and 4\. We’ve had feedback from DJs that suggests **not everyone digs our default waveform color scheme**, so we threw together the tutorial video below **demonstrating how easy it is to edit the waveform colors** in DEX 3 (using the same method, you can edit almost any of the colors in the library, including text, and VU and other items such as cue points).
