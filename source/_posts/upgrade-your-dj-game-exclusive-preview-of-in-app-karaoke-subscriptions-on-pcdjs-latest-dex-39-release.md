### An in-app Karaoke Subscription is coming to DEX 3.9 compliments of [Party Tyme Karaoke](https://www.partytyme.com/).

[Party Tyme Karaoke](https://www.partytyme.com/) is one of best-selling, best-sounding karaoke lines on the market so we’re ecstatic to be partnering-up with them.

Much like [Karaoke Cloud Pro](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) found in [PCDJ Karaoki](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), the Party Tyme karaoke catalog is **licensed for global distribution and for commercial use**. Subscribers will be able to **access over 13,000 songs** immediately with additional karaoke songs added weekly (per Party Tyme). Your cost will be **only $99-a-month**.

The Party Tyme karaoke subscription will be **accessible directly through the DEX 3 browser**. So just like [Pulselocker](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) (in-app access to 44 million non-karaoke songs) you’ll be able to search and **stream the songs AND take them offline** for use at venues that do not have internet. Users of the service will be able to easily browse the list of available titles and sort as needed.

The service will be ideal for any working DJ that’s hosting karaoke a few nights a month or a karaoke venue. **Subscribers will no longer need to source karaoke songs manually** across a hoard of karaoke websites, navigating poor karaoke recreations along the way — instead benefiting from **automatic in-app delivery** via [DJ mixing software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) they already use.

**DEX 3 is designed for today’s versatile DJ**. Now with coming karaoke subscription you can host karaoke with confidence knowing you have **the best karaoke selection** for professional use available.
